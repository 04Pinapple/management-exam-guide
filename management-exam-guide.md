## 第 1 章：管理基础 | Chapter 1: Management Foundations

### 1. 组织与管理 | Organizations and management

- **组织（organization）**：由两人或多人有意识地协调组成，为实现共同目标而开展活动的实体。

  **Organization:** A deliberately coordinated group of two or more people formed to achieve a common goal.
- **管理（management）**：通过协调和监督他人的工作，使组织目标既有效率又有效果地实现。

  **Management:** Coordinating and overseeing other people’s work so that organizational goals are achieved efficiently and effectively.
- 管理者不一定只做监督工作；他们也可能直接参与专业或执行任务。

  Managers are not limited to supervising others; they may also perform technical or operational tasks themselves.

### 2. 效率与效果 | Efficiency and effectiveness

| 概念 Concept       | 核心问题 Key question                               | 关注点 Focus                                  | 记忆提示 Reminder                                   |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 效率 Efficiency    | 怎样把事情做对？ How can the work be done right?    | 资源投入、成本、过程 Resources, cost, process | 少投入、少浪费 Use fewer resources and reduce waste |
| 效果 Effectiveness | 是否做对了该做的事？ Are we doing the right things? | 是否达成目标 Goal attainment                  | 达成目标 Achieve the goals                          |

用更低成本生产同样的产品，提高效率；生产出更高质量的产品，提高效果；若同时降成本、提质量，则两者都提高。

Producing the same product at lower cost improves efficiency. Producing a better-quality product improves effectiveness. Doing both improves efficiency and effectiveness.

### 3. 管理职能 | Management functions

1. **计划（planning）**：确定目标、制定策略和行动方案。**Planning:** Setting goals and deciding on strategies and actions to achieve them
2. **组织（organizing）**：安排工作、分配资源、设计汇报关系。**Organizing:** Arranging tasks, allocating resources, and establishing reporting relationships.
3. **领导（leading）**：激励、沟通、影响和指导员工。**Leading:** Motivating, communicating with, influencing, and guiding employees.
4. **控制（controlling）**：衡量实际表现，与目标比较并纠正偏差。
   **Controlling:** Measuring actual performance, comparing it with goals, and correcting deviations.

### 4. 管理层级与技能 | Management levels and skills

- **高层管理者（top managers）**决定组织方向、总体战略和总体结构；概念技能尤其重要。**Top managers** set the organization’s overall direction, strategy, and structure; conceptual skills are especially important.
- **中层管理者（middle managers）**承接高层目标、协调部门，并将战略转化为计划。**Middle managers** translate top-level goals into plans and coordinate departments.
- **基层管理者（first-line managers）**直接监督日常工作；技术技能通常更重要。**First-line managers** directly supervise day-to-day work; technical skills are often especially important.
- **技术技能（technical skills）**：运用特定专业知识和方法完成任务。**Technical skills:** The ability to use specialized knowledge and methods to perform tasks.
- **人际技能（human skills）**：与个人和团队沟通、合作、激励。**Human skills:** The ability to communicate, cooperate, and motivate individuals and teams.
- **概念技能（conceptual skills）**：理解组织整体、复杂关系和长远影响。**Conceptual skills:** The ability to understand the organization as a whole, complex relationships, and long-term consequences.
- 人际技能对各层级都重要；层级越高，概念技能通常越关键。
  Human skills matter at every management level; conceptual skills generally become more important at higher levels.

### 5. Mintzberg 管理者角色 | Mintzberg’s managerial roles

**人际关系角色 Interpersonal roles**

- **挂名首脑（figurehead）**：出席典礼、接待来宾、正式代表组织。**Figurehead:** Performs ceremonial duties and formally represents the organization.
- **领导者（leader）**：招聘、指导、激励和评价下属。**Leader:** Hires, guides, motivates, and evaluates employees.
- **联络者（liaison）**：维护组织内外联系、建立网络。
  **Liaison:** Maintains internal and external contacts and networks.

**信息角色 Informational roles**

- **监听者（monitor）**：收集信息、关注内外环境。**Monitor:** Collects information and scans the internal and external environment.
- **传播者（disseminator）**：向组织内部传递信息。**Disseminator:** Shares information with people inside the organization.
- **发言人（spokesperson）**：代表组织向外发布信息。
  **Spokesperson:** Communicates information on behalf of the organization to external parties.

**决策角色 Decisional roles**

- **企业家（entrepreneur）**：发起改进和变革。**Entrepreneur:** Initiates improvement and change.
- **混乱处理者（disturbance handler）**：处理冲突、危机和突发问题。**Disturbance handler:** Responds to conflicts, crises, and unexpected problems.
- **资源分配者（resource allocator）**：决定预算、人员、时间等资源如何分配。**Resource allocator:** Decides how budgets, people, time, and other resources are distributed.
- **谈判者（negotiator）**：代表组织协商重要协议。
  **Negotiator:** Represents the organization in important negotiations.

**情境提示 Scenario cues：**出席员工退休宴或代表组织参加仪式，通常是挂名首脑；指导员工工作是领导者；审阅报告收集信息是监听者；决定追加人手是资源分配者；解决突发设备故障是混乱处理者。

Attending a retirement party or representing the organization at a ceremony usually signals the figurehead role. Guiding employees signals the leader role; reviewing reports to gather information signals the monitor role; assigning extra staff signals the resource allocator role; resolving an unexpected equipment failure signals the disturbance-handler role.

### 6. 管理思想与 Taylor | Management thought and Taylor

科学管理（scientific management）用系统方法分析工作、提高生产率，而不是让员工各自依习惯采用不同做法。重点包括：

Scientific management uses systematic analysis to improve productivity rather than allowing everyone to work by personal habit. Key ideas include:

- 科学分析任务，寻找较优工作方法和标准。Analyze tasks scientifically to identify better methods and standards.
- 根据岗位选择和培训员工，并使工作方法标准化。Select and train workers for jobs and standardize work methods.
- 管理者与员工合作，确保科学方法落实。Managers and workers cooperate to put the methods into practice.
- 关注生产率、工作方法和资源使用效率。
  Focus on productivity, work methods, and efficient use of resources.

**常见陷阱 Common trap：**Taylor 关注低效率、随意的工作方法和员工产出潜力未被发挥；他并非主张员工只应承担高层工作。

Taylor was concerned with inefficiency, unsystematic work methods, and unrealized worker productivity—not with the idea that employees should perform only high-level tasks.

### 7. Fayol 的管理职能 | Fayol’s managerial functions

Henri Fayol 将管理视为一种可系统学习的活动，并概括出五项管理职能：

Henri Fayol viewed management as a systematic activity that could be taught and identified five managerial functions:

1. **计划（planning）**：预见未来并制定行动方案，以实现组织目标。**Planning:** Looking ahead and developing a course of action to achieve organizational goals.
2. **组织（organizing）**：安排人员、任务和资源，建立完成工作的结构。**Organizing:** Arranging people, tasks, and resources and establishing a structure for the work.
3. **指挥（commanding）**：指导员工开展工作并履行职责。**Commanding:** Directing employees so they carry out their work and responsibilities.
4. **协调（coordinating）**：使不同部门和活动相互配合，确保工作朝共同目标推进。**Coordinating:** Harmonizing the work of different departments and activities so they support common goals.
5. **控制（controlling）**：检查工作是否符合计划和既定标准，并在必要时纠正偏差。**Controlling:** Checking that work conforms to plans and standards and correcting deviations when necessary.

**与现代四项职能的关系 Relationship to the modern four-function framework：**许多现代教材将管理职能概括为计划、组织、领导和控制。Fayol 的“指挥”和“协调”在现代框架中通常并入“领导”（指导、沟通和激励员工）及“组织”（协调工作和资源）等职能。因此，若题目问 Fayol 的职能，应列出五项；若问现代四项管理职能，则答计划、组织、领导和控制。

Many modern textbooks summarize management as planning, organizing, leading, and controlling. Fayol’s “commanding” and “coordinating” are generally incorporated into modern functions such as leading (directing, communicating with, and motivating employees) and organizing (coordinating work and resources). Therefore, list all five when asked for Fayol’s functions; use planning, organizing, leading, and controlling when asked for the modern four-function framework.

## 第 2 章：决策与问题解决 | Chapter 2: Decision Making and Problem Solving

### 1. 理性决策过程 | Rational decision-making process

1. 识别问题 Identify the problem.
2. 确定决策标准 Identify the decision criteria.
3. 为标准分配权重 Assign weights to the criteria.
4. 提出备选方案 Develop alternatives.
5. 分析备选方案 Analyze alternatives.
6. 选择方案 Select an alternative.
7. 实施方案 Implement the alternative.
8. 评价决策效果 Evaluate the decision’s effectiveness.

**考题识别线索 Exam cues：**

- 列出购买电脑时看重的性能、价格等，是确定标准。Listing desired computer features and price is identifying criteria.
- 给性能、价格分配重要程度，是分配权重。Assigning importance to performance and price is weighting criteria.
- 列出五种电脑型号，是提出备选方案。Listing five computer models is developing alternatives.
- 比较新旧设备的实际表现，是实施后的效果评价。
  Comparing the new computers with the old ones after purchase is evaluating the decision.

### 2. 理性模型、有限理性与直觉 | Rationality, bounded rationality, and intuition

- **理性决策（rational decision making）**假设问题清楚、信息充分、方案及后果可知，决策者能一致地比较方案并选择最优方案；现实中这些假设往往不完全成立。**Rational decision making** assumes a clear problem, complete information, known alternatives and consequences, and a consistent choice of the optimal option. These assumptions are rarely fully met in practice.
- **有限理性（bounded rationality）**：人的时间、信息和认知能力有限，只能在可理解、可行的范围内寻找方案。**Bounded rationality:** Because time, information, and cognitive capacity are limited, decision makers search within a manageable set of options.
- **满意解（satisficing）**：选择第一个达到可接受标准的方案，而不是继续寻找绝对最优方案。**Satisficing:** Choosing the first acceptable option rather than continuing to search for the theoretically optimal one.
- **直觉决策（intuitive decision making）**：依靠经验、模式识别和潜意识判断；可辅助分析，但不等同于毫无依据地猜测。**Intuitive decision making:** Using experience, pattern recognition, and subconscious judgment; it can complement analysis but is not baseless guessing.
- **承诺升级（escalation of commitment）**：已有证据显示决策不理想，仍因既有投入、面子或不愿承认错误而继续投入。**Escalation of commitment:** Continuing to invest in a failing course of action because of prior investment, pride, or reluctance to admit an error.
- **启发式（heuristic）**：用简化规则快速判断，节省时间但可能造成系统性偏差。
  **Heuristic:** A simplifying rule that speeds up judgment but may create systematic bias.

#### 常见决策偏差与错误 | Common decision-making biases and errors

决策偏差是影响判断的系统性倾向。熟悉各偏差的“判断线索”，有助于在选择方案前检查证据和假设。

Decision-making biases are systematic tendencies that distort judgment. Recognizing their cues helps decision makers check evidence and assumptions before choosing.

| 偏差 Bias                                     | 含义与识别线索 Meaning and cue                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **过度自信（overconfidence）**          | 高估自己的判断、知识或预测准确性。**Overestimating one’s judgment, knowledge, or predictive accuracy.**                                                                                                            |
| **即时满足（immediate gratification）** | 偏好眼前回报，低估延迟成本或长期后果。**Preferring immediate rewards while underestimating delayed costs or long-term consequences.**                                                                               |
| **锚定（anchoring）**                   | 过度依赖最先获得的信息或数字，之后即使有新证据也调整不足。**Relying too heavily on the first information or number encountered and adjusting insufficiently when new evidence appears.**                            |
| **选择性知觉（selective perception）**  | 让既有兴趣、经验或期待影响自己注意到和解释的信息。**Allowing existing interests, experience, or expectations to shape which information is noticed and how it is interpreted.**                                     |
| **确认偏差（confirmation）**            | 寻找或重视支持既有看法的证据，同时忽略反面证据。**Seeking or emphasizing evidence that supports an existing belief while discounting contrary evidence.**                                                           |
| **框架效应（framing）**                 | 因信息呈现方式或措辞不同而改变判断，即使实质选择相同。**Changing a judgment because of how information or options are presented, even when the substance is equivalent.**                                           |
| **可得性偏差（availability）**          | 依据容易想起的近期、鲜明事件估计其发生概率，而非依据总体数据。**Estimating likelihood from examples that come readily to mind, often recent or vivid ones, rather than from overall data.**                         |
| **代表性偏差（representation）**        | 根据某对象与典型形象的相似程度作判断，忽视基础概率或样本信息。**Judging by similarity to a typical image while overlooking base rates or sample information.**                                                      |
| **随机性偏差（randomness）**            | 在随机事件中误认规律，或错误地认为短期结果必须“平衡”回来。**Seeing patterns in random events or wrongly assuming short-term outcomes must soon “balance out.”**                                                 |
| **沉没成本偏差（sunk-cost）**           | 因已经投入的时间、金钱或精力而继续一项不再值得的方案；与承诺升级密切相关。**Continuing an option that is no longer worthwhile because of resources already invested; closely related to escalation of commitment.** |
| **自利偏差（self-serving）**            | 将成功归因于自身能力，把失败归咎于外部因素。**Attributing successes to one’s own ability and failures to external causes.**                                                                                        |
| **事后聪明偏差（hindsight）**           | 结果发生后，误以为自己事先就能准确预测结果（“我早就知道”）。**After an outcome occurs, believing one had accurately predicted it beforehand (“I knew it all along”).**                                          |

**易混提醒 Quick distinctions：**确认偏差是偏向支持已有信念的证据；选择性知觉是既有期待影响注意和解释；可得性偏差依赖容易想起的例子；代表性偏差依赖与典型形象的相似度；锚定偏差则是被最初信息或数字牵住。

Confirmation bias favors evidence supporting an existing belief; selective perception lets expectations shape attention and interpretation; availability relies on examples that are easy to recall; representation relies on similarity to a stereotype or prototype; anchoring gives excessive weight to an initial piece of information or number.

**减少偏差的方法 Ways to reduce bias：**明确决策标准并事先设定权重；主动寻找反面证据，邀请独立评估者或“反方”；用基准率和可靠数据检验直觉；比较多个方案及其长期后果；区分未来成本与已经无法收回的沉没成本；在结果出现前记录预测，避免事后偏差。

Define criteria and weights before evaluating options; actively seek disconfirming evidence and invite independent reviewers or a devil’s advocate; test intuition against base rates and reliable data; compare multiple alternatives and long-term consequences; distinguish future costs from sunk costs; and record predictions before outcomes are known to limit hindsight bias.

### 3. 问题类型与决策类型 | Problem and decision types

| 问题 Problem                                   | 特征 Characteristics                                                                                               | 常对应决策 Typical decision                      |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **结构化问题（structured problem）**     | 清楚、重复、可定义，通常有已知程序 Clear, repetitive, well-defined, and often covered by a known procedure         | **程序化决策（programmed decision）**      |
| **非结构化问题（unstructured problem）** | 新颖、复杂、信息不完整、没有现成程序 Novel, complex, information is incomplete, and no ready-made procedure exists | **非程序化决策（nonprogrammed decision）** |

日常重复的采购或排班较可能是结构化问题；首次进入新市场、处理新型危机或设计全新产品，较可能是非结构化问题。高层管理者通常更常面对非程序化决策。

Routine purchasing or scheduling is more likely to be structured. Entering a new market, handling a novel crisis, or designing a completely new product is more likely to be unstructured. Top managers tend to make more nonprogrammed decisions.

### 4. 决策条件 | Decision conditions

- **确定性（certainty）**：每种方案的结果基本已知。**Certainty:** The outcome of each alternative is known.
- **风险（risk）**：结果不确定，但可依据数据或经验估计其概率。**Risk:** Outcomes are uncertain, but their probabilities can be estimated using data or experience.
- **不确定性（uncertainty）**：信息不足，难以确定结果，也难以合理估计各种结果的概率。
  **Uncertainty:** Information is insufficient to know outcomes or reasonably estimate their probabilities.

### 5. 群体决策 | Group decision making

**优势 Advantages：**信息和观点更多、备选方案更广、较易取得执行者接受、成员可共同理解决策理由。

Groups can pool more information and perspectives, generate more alternatives, gain greater acceptance from implementers, and help members understand the rationale for a decision.

**不足 Disadvantages：**耗时；可能被少数强势成员主导；责任分散；追求一致可能导致群体迷思；群体压力会压低异议。

Groups can be time-consuming, dominated by a few strong voices, diffuse responsibility, and suppress disagreement through pressure for consensus, potentially leading to groupthink.

**降低群体迷思的方法 Ways to reduce groupthink：**领导者先不表态并鼓励质疑；指定反方角色；引入外部专家；让小组独立评估；安排第二轮复核。

Leaders can withhold their own views initially, invite criticism, assign a devil’s advocate, consult outsiders, have subgroups evaluate options independently, and conduct a second review.

常见方法 Common techniques:

- **头脑风暴（brainstorming）**：先尽量生成点子，暂缓批评。Generate as many ideas as possible before evaluating or criticizing them.
- **名义群体技术（nominal group technique）**：成员先独立提出想法，再轮流分享、讨论和排序。Members first generate ideas independently, then share, discuss, and rank them in turn.
- **电子会议（electronic meetings）**：通过数字工具收集、讨论或匿名评价意见。
  Use digital tools to collect, discuss, or anonymously evaluate ideas.

### 6. 创造力与决策 | Creativity and decision making

创造力是产生新颖且有用的想法。它扩大备选方案并帮助找到新解法。决策质量不仅取决于信息，也取决于问题定义、标准权重和是否真正比较了不同方案。

Creativity is the ability to generate novel and useful ideas. It expands the alternatives available and helps solve problems in new ways. Decision quality depends not only on information but also on how the problem is defined, how criteria are weighted, and whether alternatives are genuinely compared.

## 第 3 章：当代管理议题 | Chapter 3: Contemporary Management Issues

本章试题将全球化、人口与劳动力变化、社会责任、伦理和文化差异作为综合管理议题。

Questions in this chapter combine globalization, demographic and workforce change, social responsibility, ethics, and cultural differences.

### 1. 人口变化与劳动力 | Demographic and workforce change

- **人口统计（demographics）**包括年龄、性别、种族/族群、收入、教育等人口特征及其变化。**Demographics** include population characteristics and trends such as age, gender, race or ethnicity, income, and education.
- 组织要关注劳动力构成、代际差异、人才供应、员工价值观和客户群体变化。Organizations should monitor workforce composition, generational differences, talent supply, employee values, and changes in customer groups.
- 人口结构变化会影响招聘、产品需求、服务设计和管理方式。Demographic change affects recruitment, product demand, service design, and management practices.
- 不要将群体趋势机械套用到每一个人；应尊重差异并依据事实管理。
  Do not apply group-level trends mechanically to individuals; respect differences and manage based on evidence.

### 2. 跨文化管理 | Cross-cultural management

试题常以员工的表达方式、赞扬反应、家庭观念、上下级关系或风险态度为情境，要求判断文化维度。

Questions often use scenarios about communication, reactions to praise, family priorities, hierarchy, or attitudes toward risk to test cultural dimensions.

- **权力距离（power distance）**：社会成员对权力不平等及组织等级（rank）差异的接受程度。它关注的不只是组织里有没有级别，而是人们是否认为不同等级拥有不同权威、地位和待遇是正常且可接受的。**Power distance:** The extent to which members of a society accept unequal distributions of power and differences in organizational rank. It concerns not merely whether levels exist, but whether people regard different authority, status, and privileges across ranks as normal and acceptable.

  - **高权力距离（high power distance）**：较能接受明确的等级和上下级权力差异，尊重职位权威；下属通常较少公开质疑上级，决策倾向由高层作出，沟通较正式、由上而下。职位等级可能伴随不同的待遇或特权，且被视为合理。**High power distance:** People are more accepting of clear hierarchy and unequal authority, defer to positional rank, and are less likely to openly challenge superiors. Decisions tend to be made at higher levels, communication is more formal and top-down, and rank-based differences in status or privileges may be viewed as appropriate.
  - **低权力距离（low power distance）**：更强调平等和较小的等级差异；员工较期待参与决策、直接与主管沟通并提出异议，管理关系相对非正式，权威通常需要解释和讨论。**Low power distance:** People place greater emphasis on equality and smaller status differences. Employees are more likely to expect participation, communicate directly with managers, and question decisions; relationships are less formal, and authority is more open to explanation and discussion.

  **判断线索 Rank and power-distance cues：**题目若强调员工因职位低而不敢反对、重要决定由高层集中作出，或高职位理应享有特殊待遇，通常体现高权力距离；若强调员工可直接称呼主管、公开表达异议、平等参与和分散决策，通常体现低权力距离。不要把“组织有正式职级”本身当作高权力距离的充分证据，关键是成员对等级不平等的接受程度。**If a scenario emphasizes that lower-ranking employees hesitate to disagree, major decisions are centralized at the top, or senior rank deserves special privileges, it generally signals high power distance. Direct access to managers, open disagreement, equal participation, and decentralized decisions generally signal low power distance. The mere existence of formal job titles is not enough to establish high power distance; the key is how acceptable rank-based inequality is to members.**
- **不确定性规避（uncertainty avoidance）**：对模糊、不确定情境的不适程度。高规避偏好规则、可预测性和充分信息；低规避更能容忍模糊与试错。**Uncertainty avoidance:** The degree of discomfort with ambiguity. High uncertainty avoidance favors rules, predictability, and information; low uncertainty avoidance is more tolerant of ambiguity and experimentation.
- **个人主义 / 集体主义（individualism / collectivism）**：个人目标与自主，或群体归属、关系和共同利益的相对重要性。**Individualism / collectivism:** The relative emphasis on personal autonomy and goals versus group belonging, relationships, and shared interests.
- **成就 / 关怀取向（achievement / nurturing orientation）**：竞争、成就和绩效，或关系、生活质量与关怀的相对强调。**Achievement / nurturing orientation:** The relative emphasis on competition and performance versus relationships, quality of life, and care.
- **未来取向（future orientation）**：重视长期规划、延迟满足和未来回报的程度。**Future orientation:** The extent to which long-term planning, delayed gratification, and future rewards are valued.
- **绩效取向（performance orientation）**：社会鼓励并奖励改进、卓越和高绩效的程度。**Performance orientation:** The extent to which a society encourages and rewards improvement, excellence, and high performance.
- 文化线索用于理解管理环境，不应用来给个人贴标签。
  Cultural dimensions help explain management contexts; they should not be used to stereotype individuals.

### 3. 社会责任与可持续发展 | Social responsibility and sustainability

| 立场 View                                    | 核心观点 Core idea                                                                                                 | 判断提示 Cue                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **传统/古典观点（classical view）**    | 企业首要责任是盈利并遵守法律 A firm’s primary responsibility is to earn profits while obeying the law.            | 将其他社会目标视为偏离利润目标 Treats other social goals as a diversion from profit.              |
| **社会经济观点（socioeconomic view）** | 企业还应回应社会利益和更广泛的利益相关者 Firms should also address social interests and broader stakeholder needs. | 企业的资源和影响力带来相应责任 A firm’s resources and influence create broader responsibilities. |

- **社会义务（social obligation）**：达到法律和经济责任要求的最低限度。**Social obligation:** Meeting the minimum legal and economic responsibilities.
- **社会回应（social responsiveness）**：对社会需求作出反应，通常回应既有压力或期待。**Social responsiveness:** Responding to social needs, pressures, or expectations.
- **社会责任（social responsibility）**：主动采取有益社会的行动，超越最低法律要求。**Social responsibility:** Taking actions that benefit society beyond minimum legal requirements.
- **可持续发展（sustainability）**：兼顾长期经营可行性、社会影响和环境承载能力，而非只看短期利润或单一环保指标。
  **Sustainability:** Balancing long-term business viability, social impact, and environmental capacity rather than focusing only on short-term profit or a single environmental measure.

社会参与不必然带来巨大财务收益或损失；应根据情境、时间跨度、利益相关者和经营方式分析。

Social involvement does not automatically produce huge financial gains or losses. Evaluate it in context, over an appropriate time horizon, and across stakeholders and business practices.

### 4. 管理伦理 | Managerial ethics

伦理是判断行为对错的原则。常见判断框架：

Ethics consists of principles for distinguishing right from wrong. Common ethical frameworks include:

- **功利主义（utilitarian view）**：选择总体净收益最大、总体伤害最小的方案；可能牺牲少数人的利益。**Utilitarian view:** Choose the action that produces the greatest overall benefit and least overall harm, though minority interests may be sacrificed.
- **权利观（rights view）**：保护个人基本权利，如隐私、表达、公平程序和免受伤害。**Rights view:** Protect individual rights such as privacy, free expression, due process, and freedom from harm.
- **正义观（justice view）**：公平、一致地分配利益与负担，并采用公正程序。
  **Justice view:** Distribute benefits and burdens fairly and use fair procedures.

组织层面常考 Organizational issues:

- **伦理规范（code of ethics）**以书面形式说明核心价值和预期行为，但单靠规范不能保证员工合乎伦理。A **code of ethics** states organizational values and expected conduct, but a written code alone cannot ensure ethical behavior.
- 管理者行为会为员工树立规范；领导者持续不道德可能带坏组织风气。Managers set behavioral examples; persistently unethical leaders can undermine the organization’s ethical climate.
- 伦理文化需要领导示范、沟通、培训、咨询/举报渠道、公平奖惩和实际问责。An ethical culture requires leadership by example, communication, training, advice/reporting channels, fair rewards and penalties, and real accountability.
- 分析关厂、外包、裁员或环境案例时，同时考虑股东、员工、消费者、社区、法律责任及长短期后果。
  For plant closures, outsourcing, layoffs, or environmental cases, consider shareholders, employees, customers, communities, legal duties, and short- and long-term consequences.

### 5. 组织文化 | Organizational culture

- **组织文化（organizational culture）**：成员共同持有并影响其行为方式的基本价值观、信念和理解。**Organizational culture:** Shared values, beliefs, and understandings that influence how members behave.
- **强文化（strong culture）**：核心价值观被广泛认同且影响力强，成员较容易理解组织重视什么。**Strong culture:** Core values are widely shared and strongly influence behavior, making it clear what the organization values.
- **弱文化（weak culture）**：价值观不够一致或影响有限，成员行为更依赖个人、团队或正式规则。**Weak culture:** Values are less consistently shared or influential, so behavior depends more on individuals, teams, or formal rules.
- 强文化能协调行为、增强认同；若过于固化，也可能阻碍变革或排斥不同观点。A strong culture can coordinate behavior and strengthen identity, but a rigid culture may block change or exclude differing views.
- 文化影响计划、风险取舍、招聘、奖励、创新和伦理决策。Culture shapes planning, risk taking, hiring, rewards, innovation, and ethical decisions.
- 管理者可通过以身作则、招聘与甄选、培训与社会化、奖励制度、故事/仪式和持续沟通塑造文化。Managers shape culture through role modeling, recruitment and selection, training and socialization, reward systems, stories and rituals, and ongoing communication.
- **创新文化**鼓励新想法、试验、跨部门交流和学习；**伦理文化**明确可接受行为并一致问责；**可持续文化**把长期社会和环境影响纳入日常决策。
  An **innovation culture** encourages new ideas, experimentation, cross-functional exchange, and learning. An **ethical culture** defines acceptable conduct and applies accountability consistently. A **sustainability culture** incorporates long-term social and environmental effects into everyday decisions.

## 第 4 章：全球环境与国际管理 | Chapter 4: The Global Environment and International Management

### 1. 外部环境 | The external environment

- **具体环境（specific environment）**：直接影响组织日常经营的利益相关者，如顾客、供应商、竞争者、监管机构和劳动力市场。**Specific environment:** Stakeholders that directly affect day-to-day operations, such as customers, suppliers, competitors, regulators, and labor markets.
- **一般环境（general environment）**包括：**General environment** includes:
  - 经济 Economic：利率、通胀、增长、收入与消费。Interest rates, inflation, economic growth, income, and consumption.
  - 人口统计 Demographic：年龄、人口结构、人口迁移、劳动力组成。Age, population structure, migration, and workforce composition.
  - 社会文化 Sociocultural：价值观、生活方式和社会规范。Values, lifestyles, and social norms.
  - 政治/法律 Political/legal：政府政策、法律、监管和政治稳定性。Government policies, laws, regulation, and political stability.
  - 技术 Technological：新技术、自动化、数字平台与技术变迁。New technologies, automation, digital platforms, and technological change.
  - 全球 Global：跨国竞争、供应链、国际冲突、汇率和跨境规则。
    International competition, supply chains, global conflict, exchange rates, and cross-border rules.

**情境判断 Scenario cues：**法规或法院裁决属于政治/法律；消费者价值观变化属于社会文化；自然灾害造成跨国供应链中断涉及全球环境，并可能影响经济或生产。

A law or court ruling is political/legal; changing consumer values are sociocultural; a natural disaster disrupting an international supply chain involves the global environment and may also affect economic activity or production.

### 2. 环境不确定性与扫描 | Environmental uncertainty and scanning

- 环境不确定性通常随**环境复杂度**（因素的数量和差异）及**变化速度**（稳定或动态）增加。Environmental uncertainty generally rises with **complexity** (the number and diversity of factors) and **rate of change** (stable versus dynamic conditions).
- **环境扫描（environmental scanning）**持续收集外部信息，以识别趋势和潜在威胁。**Environmental scanning** is the ongoing collection of external information to identify trends and potential threats.
- 竞争情报应以合法、合乎伦理的方式收集，如分析公开资料、购买公开销售的竞争产品或参加公开展会；不得违法获取商业秘密。Competitive intelligence should be gathered legally and ethically—for example, by analyzing public information, buying publicly available products, or attending public trade shows—not by illegally obtaining trade secrets.
- 定期复核假设，避免因为过去成功就假设未来环境不变。
  Review assumptions regularly; past success does not guarantee that the environment will remain unchanged.

### 3. 管理者影响绩效的两种观点 | Two views of managerial impact

- **全能观（omnipotent view）**：管理者对组织成败负有主要责任，强调管理决策和行动的作用。**Omnipotent view:** Managers are primarily responsible for organizational success or failure; managerial actions and decisions strongly shape outcomes.
- **象征观（symbolic view）**：组织结果受经济、竞争、政策等外部因素显著制约，管理者控制有限。
  **Symbolic view:** Organizational outcomes are substantially constrained by external forces such as the economy, competition, and policy, limiting managerial control.

若题干强调领导者能直接控制结果，偏全能观；若强调不可控外部环境，偏象征观。

If a scenario emphasizes that leaders directly control outcomes, it points to the omnipotent view. If it emphasizes uncontrollable external forces, it points to the symbolic view.

### 4. 国际经营方式与组织类型 | International business and organization types

国际经营方式通常从投入较低到控制较强包括：

International involvement generally ranges from lower commitment to greater control:

1. **进出口（exporting / importing）**
2. **合同方式（contractual arrangements）**，如许可经营（licensing）和特许经营（franchising）
3. **战略联盟或合资企业（strategic alliance / joint venture）**
4. **建立或收购外国子公司（foreign subsidiary）**

其他术语 Other terms:

- **全球采购（global sourcing）**：在全球寻找原料、零部件或服务来源。**Global sourcing:** Obtaining materials, components, or services from sources around the world.
- **近岸/离岸安排（nearshoring / offshoring）**：把业务安排到邻近国家，或迁至其他国家；需考虑成本、能力、风险、劳工和社会影响。**Nearshoring / offshoring:** Locating operations in a nearby country / another country; consider cost, capability, risk, labor, and social impact.
- 国际经营还需考虑文化、法律、人才、物流、政治和汇率风险。
  International operations must also account for cultural, legal, talent, logistics, political, and exchange-rate risks.

### 5. 文化与国际管理 | Culture and international management

管理方式应适应当地法律、价值观、沟通习惯和工作关系。不要把直接表达一概视为冒犯、把重视关系视为缺乏绩效，或把重视规则视为缺乏主动性；应先理解文化情境，再结合个体事实处理。

Management practices should fit local laws, values, communication norms, and work relationships. Do not automatically interpret directness as rudeness, relationship orientation as poor performance, or rule orientation as a lack of initiative. Understand the cultural context and assess individuals on their own evidence.

**未来取向（future orientation）**指社会鼓励和奖励规划未来、延迟满足及为未来投资的程度。它是一个连续维度，不是对某个国家或个人的固定标签。

**Future orientation** is the extent to which a society encourages and rewards planning for the future, delaying gratification, and investing for future returns. It is a continuum, not a fixed label for a country or an individual.

| 取向 Orientation                                                               | 常见倾向 Typical tendencies                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | 管理情境中的线索 Management cues                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **高未来取向 High future orientation**                                   | 重视长期目标、储蓄和投资、提前规划、延迟眼前回报以换取长期收益；更愿意评估长期影响。**Values long-term goals, saving and investment, advance planning, and delaying immediate rewards for future gains; gives greater weight to long-term consequences.** | 组织可能更支持长期战略、员工发展和培训投资，并以长期绩效衡量结果；员工可能接受为未来收益而进行的短期投入。**Organizations may favor long-range strategy, employee development, and training investment, and evaluate results over longer periods; employees may accept short-term investment for future returns.** |

**Exam cue:** Long-range planning, saving or investing, future returns from training, and willingness to delay gratification generally indicate high future orientation. Emphasis on immediate rewards, instant gratification, or short-term results generally indicates low future orientation. Low future orientation does not mean irresponsibility or an inability to plan; it means that distant-future concerns receive relatively less cultural emphasis.

#### 第 5 章：组织变革与创新 | Chapter 5: Organizational Change and Innovation

### 1. 变革的来源与类型 | Forces and types of change

- **外部推动力 External forces:** 竞争、技术、法律法规、经济波动、顾客需求、社会趋势、劳动力市场和全球环境。Competition, technology, laws and regulation, economic shifts, customer needs, social trends, labor markets, and global forces.
- **内部推动力 Internal forces:** 战略调整、管理层更换、员工态度或技能变化、设备更新、绩效问题和组织结构调整。
  Strategic shifts, leadership changes, employee attitudes or skills, new equipment, performance problems, and structural adjustments.

| 变革类型 Type                           | 典型内容 Typical examples                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **结构变革 Structural change**    | 汇报关系、部门、管理层级、权责和控制幅度 Reporting relationships, departments, hierarchy, authority, and span of control |
| **技术变革 Technological change** | 新设备、软件、生产方法、流程或工作技术 New equipment, software, production methods, processes, or work technology        |
| **人员变革 People change**        | 员工态度、技能、行为、关系或文化 Employee attitudes, skills, behavior, relationships, or culture                         |

**记忆提示 Memory aid：**改变“谁向谁汇报”是结构；引进新软件是技术；改变员工态度和能力是人员。

Changing who reports to whom is structural; introducing new software is technological; changing employee attitudes or capabilities is a people change.

### 2. Lewin 三阶段变革模型 | Lewin’s three-step change model

1. **解冻（unfreezing）**：打破旧有平衡，使成员认识到变革必要性。**Unfreezing:** Disrupt the status quo and create awareness of the need for change.
2. **改变/移动（changing/moving）**：试行并实施新的流程、行为或结构。**Changing/moving:** Introduce and implement new processes, behaviors, or structures.
3. **再冻结（refreezing）**：通          过制度、培训、激励和文化巩固新做法。
   **Refreezing:** Reinforce the new practices through systems, training, rewards, and culture.

力量场分析（force-field analysis）识别推动变革与阻碍变革的力量；可增强推动力，或降低阻力。

**Force-field analysis** identifies forces for and against change. Managers can strengthen driving forces or reduce restraining forces.

- **平静水面隐喻（calm waters）**：变革较少、相对可预测，组织在稳定时期中偶尔经历变革。**Calm waters metaphor:** Change is occasional and predictable, with periods of stability in between.
- **激流险滩隐喻（white-water rapids）**：环境持续快速变化，组织必须不断调整。
  **White-water rapids metaphor:** The environment changes continuously and rapidly, requiring ongoing adaptation.

### 3. 克服变革阻力 | Overcoming resistance to change

- **沟通和教育（communication and education）**：解释原因、影响和计划。Explain the reasons, impact, and plan.
- **参与（participation）**：让受影响员工参与设计与实施。Involve affected employees in planning and implementation.
- **支持（support）**：提供培训、辅导、资源和心理支持。Provide training, coaching, resources, and emotional support.
- **谈判（negotiation）**：与确有损失的一方协商补偿。Negotiate with parties who face real losses.
- **操纵/吸纳（manipulation/co-optation）**：谨慎使用；不透明会伤害信任。Use cautiously; lack of transparency can damage trust.
- **强制（coercion）**：仅在必要且合规时使用，可能引发长期反弹。
  Use only when necessary and lawful; it may create lasting resistance.

### 4. 创造力与创新 | Creativity and innovation

- **创造力（creativity）**：产生新颖且有用的想法。**Creativity:** Generating novel and useful ideas.
- **创新（innovation）**：把创意转化为有价值的新产品、服务、流程或工作方法。**Innovation:** Turning creative ideas into valuable new products, services, processes, or work methods.
- 创意只是起点 ；只有实施并产生实际用途，才形成创新。An idea is only a starting point; it becomes innovation when implemented and put to practical use.
- 创新受个人能力与动机、组织结构、资源、领导支持、团队合作和文化氛围共同影响。Innovation is shaped by individual skills and motivation, structure, resources, leadership support, teamwork, and culture.
- **创意倡导者（idea champion）**主动支持、宣传并推动有潜力的想法落地。An **idea champion** actively supports, promotes, and advances a promising idea.
- 有利的创新环境容许适度试验，鼓励跨部门交流，提供资源，容忍诚实失败并认可贡献。
  An innovative environment permits experimentation, encourages cross-functional communication, provides resources, tolerates honest failure, and recognizes contributions.

### 5. 组织发展与创新结构 | Organizational development and structures for innovation

- 有机式、较灵活的结构通常比高度僵化的层级更利于创新。Flexible, organic structures are generally more supportive of innovation than rigid hierarchies.
- 跨职能团队、独立创新团队、资源余量、开放沟通和管理者支持有助于创意转化。Cross-functional teams, dedicated innovation teams, slack resources, open communication, and managerial support help turn ideas into practice.
- 创新不一定是革命性改变；流程、服务或产品的渐进改进也属于创新。Innovation need not be revolutionary; incremental impr  ovements to processes, services, or products also count.
- 可持续创新应同时评估用户价值、组织可行性和长期社会/环境影响。
  Sustainable innovation should consider user value, organizational feasibility, and long-term social and environmental effects.

## 第 6 章：计划与目标设定 | Chapter 6: Planning and Goal Setting

### 1. 计划的意义与局限 | The value and limits of planning

计划包括设定目标、制定实现目标的策略，以及安排行动步骤。

Planning involves setting goals, developing strategies to achieve them, and arranging the actions required.

**计划的价值 Benefits：**

- 指明方向，减少部门和个人各自为政。Provides direction and reduces conflicting efforts.
- 协调活动、资源和责任。Coordinates activities, resources, and responsibilities.
- 促使管理者预先考虑变化和风险。Encourages managers to anticipate change and risk.
- 提供衡量进度和控制结果的标准。
  Provides standards for measuring progress and controlling results.

**正式计划的局限 Limits：**

- 制定计划可能耗时、耗费资源。Planning can consume time and resources.
- 过分僵化会限制应变和创新。Excessive rigidity can constrain adaptation and innovation.
- 计划不保证绩效提升；外部变化、执行不足或员工未参与都可能使计划失效。Planning does not guarantee higher performance; environmental change, weak implementation, or lack of employee involvement may undermine it.
- 过去成功的方案不能机械沿用；须依据新环境更新。Successful past plans should not be copied blindly; update them for current conditions.
- 计划应是可调整的路线图，而不是要求组织无视变化的死规则。
  A plan should be an adaptable roadmap, not a rigid rule that ignores change.

### 2. 目标和计划的层次 | Goal and plan hierarchy

- **目标（goals）**：希望实现的结果。**Goals:** Desired outcomes.
- **计划（plans）**：说明如何实现目标的行动安排。**Plans:** Actions or arrangements that describe how goals will be achieved.
- **战略计划（strategic plans）**：面向全组织总体目标、长期方向和资源配置，通常由高层负责。**Strategic plans:** Organization-wide plans for overall goals, long-term direction, and resource allocation, usually developed by top managers.
- **战术计划（tactical plans）**：将战略转化为部门或单位的具体行动。**Tactical plans:** Translate strategy into specific actions for departments or units.
- **运营计划（operational plans）**：规定日常任务、流程、时间和执行标准。
  **Operational plans:** Specify day-to-day tasks, procedures, schedules, and performance standards.

**记忆链 Memory chain：**战略说“组织要去哪” → 战术说“部门怎样贡献” → 运营说“每天具体怎么做”。

Strategy says where the organization is going; tactics explain how departments contribute; operations specify what people do each day.



### 3. 计划分类维度 | Dimensions of plans

| 维度 Dimension                                             | 一端 One end          | 另一端 Other end                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 广度/层次                         Scope/level | 战略计划 Strategic   | 战术或运营计划 Tactical or operational |
| 时间跨度 Time frame                                        | 长期 Long-term        | 短期 Short-term                        |
| 具体程度 Specificity                                       | 具体计划 Specific     | 指导性计划 Directional                 |
| 使用次数 Frequency of use                                  | 一次性计划 Single-use | 常设计划 Standing                      |

- **具体计划（specific plans）**目标和步骤明确，解释空间较少；**指导性计划（directional plans）**给出方向并保留调整余地。**Specific plans** have clear objectives and steps with little room for interpretation; **directional plans** provide general guidelines and allow flexibility.
- **一次性计划（single-use plans）**用于独特任务或项目；**常设计划（standing plans）**用于反复工作，如政策、规则和程序。**Single-use plans** address unique projects; **standing plans** guide recurring activities through policies, rules, and procedures.
- 长期和短期的界线随组织和环境变化，并非所有组织都用同一个固定年限。
  The boundary between long-term and short-term plans varies by organization and environment; there is no universal time limit.

### 4. 目标设定与 MBO | Goal setting and MBO

传统目标设定通常由高层向下分解；若沟通不好，目标可能在传递中失真或脱离员工实际。

Traditional goal setting usually flows downward from top management. Poor communication can distort goals or disconnect them from employees’ work.

**目标管理（management by objectives, MBO）**：管理者与员工共同确定清晰、可衡量的目标，定期回顾进展，并把绩效反馈与奖励联系起来。

**Management by objectives (MBO):** Managers and employees jointly set clear, measurable goals, review progress regularly, and connect performance feedback with rewards.

典型循环 Typical cycle:

1. 复核组织使命、岗位职责和资源。Review the organization’s mission, job responsibilities, and resources.
2. 共同制定具体目标并明确期限。Jointly set specific goals and deadlines.
3. 沟通目标及衡量标准。Communicate goals and performance measures.
4. 监测进度、提供反馈，必要时修订。Monitor progress, provide feedback, and revise goals when needed.
5. 评价结果并与发展或奖励衔接。
   Evaluate results and connect them with development or rewards.

MBO 的要点不是目标容易达成，也不是只有高层设目标；关键是目标明确、参与制定、可跟踪并有反馈。

MBO is not about making goals easy or having top managers set them alone. Its key features are clear goals, participation, measurement, and feedback.

**目标链（means-ends chain）**：低层级目标应成为实现更高层级目标的手段；部门和个人目标要与组织方向一致。

A **means-ends chain** links lower-level goals as means to higher-level goals. Departmental and individual goals should support the organization’s direction.

### 5. 计划成功的关键 | Keys to successful planning

- 与组织目标和外部环境相适应。Align plans with organizational go   als and the external environment.
- 明确责任、资源、时间和衡量方法。Specify responsibilities, resources, schedules, and measures.
- 确保执行者理解并参与计划。Ensure that implementers understand and participate in the plan.
- 建立反馈、复核和调整机制。Build in feedback, review, and adjustment.
- 给执行留出合理时间；不能因为已有投入就拒绝修订失效计划。
  Allow reasonable time for implementation, but do not refuse to revise a failing plan merely because resources have already been invested.

## 第 7 章：组织结构设计 | Chapter 7: Organizational Structure and Design

### 1. 组织设计的基本要素 | Basic elements of organizational design

1. **工作专门化（work specialization）**：把工作拆分为不同任务，由不同员工负责，即劳动分工。**Work specialization:** Dividing work into separate tasks assigned to different employees; also called division of labor.
2. **部门化（departmentalization）**：按共同逻辑组合岗位。**Departmentalization:** Grouping jobs according to a common organizing principle.
3. **指挥链（chain of command）**：组织内正式的报告和权责路线。**Chain of command:** The formal line of reporting relationships and authority.
4. **控制幅度（span of control）**：管理者有效直接管理的下属数量。**Span of control:** The number of employees a manager can effectively supervise directly.
5. **集权 / 分权（centralization / decentralization）**：决策权集中在高层，还是下放到较低层级。**Centralization / decentralization:** The extent to which decision authority is concentrated at higher levels or delegated to lower levels.
6. **正规化（formalization）**：工作规则、程序和标准的明确程度。
   **Formalization:** The degree to which jobs are standardized through rules, procedures, and standards.

### 2. 部门化的类型 | Types of departmentalization

- **职能部门化（functional）**：按专业职能分，如市场、财务、人力资源。**Functional:** Groups jobs by function, such as marketing, finance, or human resources.
- **产品部门化（product）**：按产品或产品线分。**Product:** Groups jobs by product or product line.
- **地域部门化（geographic）**：按地区、国家或服务范围分。**Geographic:** Groups jobs by region, country, or territory served.
- **流程部门化（process）**：按工作流程阶段分。**Process:** Groups jobs according to stages in a work process.
- **顾客部门化（customer）**：按顾客类别或服务对象分。
  **Customer:** Groups jobs according to customer types or groups served.

识别关键是看岗位/部门按什么共同标准分组，而不是看组织名称。

To identify a type, ask what common basis is used to group the jobs or departments—not what the organization is called.

### 3. 指挥、权力与授权 | Authority, power, and delegation

- **权威（authority）**：职位赋予管理者作决定、发出指令和使用资源的正式权利。**Authority:** The formal right attached to a position to make decisions, issue orders, and use resources.
- **责任（responsibility）**：承担完成任务的义务。**Responsibility:** The obligation to complete an assigned task.                                                             
- **统一指挥（unity of command）**：传统原则要求员工只向一位直接主管负责；矩阵和项目工作可能涉及多个协调关系，因此须明确优先级和冲突处理方式。**Unity of command:** The traditional principle that an employee reports to one direct supervisor. Matrix and project work may involve multiple working relationships, so priorities and conflict-resolution procedures must be clear.
- **直线职权（line authority）**：直接指挥组织核心工作链条中的下属。**Line authority:** The right to direct subordinates in the organization’s core chain of command.
- **职能/参谋职权（staff authority）**：提供专业建议、支持或服务，通常不直接指挥核心业务线。**Staff authority:** The right to advise, support, or serve the organization, usually without directly commanding the core operating line.
- **授权（delegation）**：将任务及相应权限交给他人执行；管理者仍对整体结果负有责任。
  **Delegation:** Assigning tasks and appropriate authority to another person; the manager remains accountable for overall results.

**常见权力来源 Sources of power：**

- **合法权力 Legitimate power**：来自正式职位。Comes from a formal position.
- **奖赏权力 Reward power**：控制奖励的能力。Comes from the ability to provide rewards.
- **强制权力 Coercive power**：施加惩罚或不利后果的能力。Comes from the ability to impose penalties or undesirable consequences.
- **专家权力 Expert power**：来自专业知识和能力。Comes from specialized knowledge and skill.
- **参照权力 Referent power**：来自他人认同、尊重或愿意追随。
  Comes from others’ identification with, respect for, or desire to follow the person.

### 4. 组织结构类型 | Types of organizational structure

- **简单结构（simple structure）**：层级少、控制集中，灵活但容易过度依赖负责人。**Simple structure:** Few layers and centralized control; flexible but potentially overdependent on one leader.
- **职能结构（functional structure）**：按职能专业化，有利于专业效率，但跨部门协调可能较难。**Functional structure:** Specializes by function, promoting expertise but sometimes making cross-functional coordination difficult.
- **事业部结构（divisional structure）**：按产品、地区或顾客划分业务单元，责任清楚、反应较快，但可能重复配置资源。**Divisional structure:** Organizes units by product, geography, or customer; clarifies responsibility and can improve responsiveness, but may duplicate resources.
- **矩阵结构（matrix structure）**：职能部门与项目/产品团队并存，员工通常同时面对职能主管和项目主管。**Matrix structure:** Combines functional departments with project or product teams; employees commonly report to both functional and project managers.
- **网络结构（network structure）**：组织保留核心能力，通过外部合作或承包完成部分活动。**Network structure:** Retains core capabilities while completing some activities through external partners or contractors.
- **项目/团队型结构（team/project structure）**侧重跨职能团队完成具体任务。
  **Team/project structure** relies on cross-functional teams to complete specific tasks.

### 5. 矩阵结构：简答必备 | Matrix structure: short-answer topic

矩阵结构把职能专业线与产品、项目或客户项目线结合，员工可能同时向职能经理和项目经理汇报。

A matrix combines functional expertise with product, project, or customer-project teams. An employee may report to both a functional manager and a project manager.

- **优点 Advantages：**灵活调配专家、加强跨职能整合、促进信息共享、快速集中资源完成项目。Flexible use of specialists, stronger cross-functional integration, information sharing, and rapid resource coordination for projects.
- **缺点 Disadvantages：**双重汇报引发角色冲突、权责不清、沟通协调成本高、经理争夺资源、员工承受双重压力。Dual reporting can create role conflict and unclear authority, increase coordination costs, trigger resource competition, and place pressure on employees.
- **适用条件 Conditions：**项目复杂、环境变化快、需要不同专业协同。应提前说明权限、冲突升级路径和绩效评价归属。
  Suitable for complex projects, fast-changing environments, and work requiring diverse expertise. Clarify decision rights, escalation procedures, and performance evaluation in advance.

### 6. 机械式与有机式结构 | Mechanistic and organic structures

| 机械式 Mechanistic                                                     | 有机式 Organic                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 高度正规化和标准化 Highly formalized and standardized                  | 规则较少、流程灵活 Fewer rules and more flexible processes                      |
| 层级清楚、权力较集中 Clear hierarchy and more centralized authority    | 权力较分散、团队协作多 More decentralized authority and teamwork                |
| 信息多沿正式链条传递 Information mainly follows formal channels        | 信息横向流动、沟通开放 Information flows horizontally and communication is open |
| 适合稳定、重复、可预测任务 Suited to stable, routine, predictable work | 适合快速变化、创新或复杂任务 Suited to dynamic, innovative, or complex work     |

结构应结合战略、组织规模、技术和环境不确定性选择；不存在任何情境下都最好的单一结构。

Choose a structure based on strategy, size, technology, and environmental uncertainty; no single structure is best in every situation.

### 7. 学习型组织 | Learning organization

学习型组织持续获取、创造、共享和应用知识，并根据反馈改变行为。

A **learning organization** continually acquires, creates, shares, and applies knowledge, then changes its behavior based on feedback.

常见结构特点 Common structural characteristics:

- 决策下放，员工拥有解决问题的自主权。Decision making is decentralized and employees have autonomy to solve problems.
- 层级较少，跨职能团队和合作网络较多。There are fewer hierarchical layers and more cross-functional teams and collaborative networks.
- 信息开放，经验与知识能在团队间流动。Information is open and experience and knowledge flow among teams.
- 鼓励学习、试验、反思并共同解决问题。Learning, experimentation, reflection, and collaborative problem solving are encouraged.
- 管理者不仅下令，也促进团队学习和知识共享。
  Managers do more than give orders; they facilitate team learning and knowledge sharing.

学习型组织不要求所有员工持有完全相同的想法；共同愿景可以与开放学习并存。

A learning organization does not require everyone to think identically; a shared vision can coexist with open learning.

## 第 8 章：人力资源管理 | Chapter 8: Human Resource Management

### 1. 目标与流程 | Purpose and process

- **人力资源管理（human resource management, HRM）**：获取、培训、激励并留住胜任且表现良好的员工。**Human resource management (HRM):** Acquiring, training, motivating, and retaining competent, high-performing employees.
- HRM 不只是人力资源部门的职责；大多数管理者都会在本部门内参与人员相关决策。HRM is not solely the responsibility of the HR department; most managers make people-related decisions within their own departments.
- 典型 HRM 流程分为三个阶段：A typical HRM process has three broad stages:
  1. **获取胜任员工：**人力资源规划、招聘、甄选。**Acquire competent employees:** human resource planning, recruitment, and selection.
  2. **提供技能与知识：**入职引导、培训和员工发展。**Provide skills and knowledge:** orientation, training, and employee development.
  3. **留住高绩效员工：**绩效管理、薪酬福利、职业发展与员工关系。
     **Retain high-performing employees:** performance management, compensation and benefits, career development, and employee relations.
- **人力资源规划（employment planning）**：预测组织未来的人力需求与供给，决定需要增加还是减少员工，并安排相应人员行动。**Employment planning:** Forecasting future workforce demand and supply, deciding whether staffing needs to increase or decrease, and planning the appropriate workforce actions.
- HRM 受经营环境、劳动力市场和法律环境等外部条件影响；招聘、评价、薪酬与解雇都须遵守适用法律。
  HRM is affected by external conditions such as the business environment, labor market, and legal environment. Recruitment, evaluation, compensation, and termination must comply with applicable laws.

### 2. 岗位分析、岗位说明与岗位规范 | Job analysis, description, and specification

- **岗位分析（job analysis）**：系统检查一个岗位，确定工作任务以及完成工作所需的技能、知识、能力和行为。**Job analysis:** A systematic examination of a job to identify its tasks and the skills, knowledge, abilities, and behaviors required to perform it.
- **岗位说明书（job description）**：书面说明岗位持有人做什么、如何做、为什么做，即岗位职责和工作内容。**Job description:** A written statement of what the jobholder does, how it is done, and why—the job’s duties and responsibilities.
- **岗位规范（job specification）**：列出胜任岗位所需的资格条件，如技能、知识、经验、教育和能力。**Job specification:** A statement of the qualifications needed to perform a job, such as skills, knowledge, experience, education, and abilities.
- **人力资源清单（human resource inventory）**：记录现有员工的教育背景、专长、能力和特殊技能，用于盘点组织现有的人才供给。
  **Human resource inventory:** A record of current employees’ education, specialties, capabilities, and particular skills, used to assess the organization’s existing talent supply.

### 3. 招聘、甄选与人员规划 | Recruitment, selection, and workforce planning

- **招聘（recruitment）**：寻找并吸引可能胜任岗位的候选人。**Recruitment:** Locating and attracting candidates who may be qualified for a job.
- **甄选（selection）**：评估候选人并选择与岗位要求相符的人选。**Selection:** Assessing candidates and choosing those whose qualifications fit the job.
- 人员规划要同时考虑人力需求和人才供给；组织可能通过招聘增加员工，也可能因需求下降而缩编（downsizing）。Workforce planning considers both labor demand and talent supply. An organization may recruit to add employees or downsize when staffing demand falls.
- 多元化招聘可扩大候选人来源，例如采用有针对性的招聘渠道；单纯收窄候选人来源可能减少多样性。Diversity-focused recruitment can broaden the candidate pool through targeted outreach. Simply narrowing recruitment sources may reduce diversity.
- **平权行动（affirmative action）**旨在改善受保护群体获得就业机会的状况；应与反歧视法律、岗位资格和公平甄选程序一并理解。
  **Affirmative action** aims to improve employment opportunities for protected groups. It should be understood alongside antidiscrimination law, job qualifications, and fair selection procedures.

### 4. 劳动力多元化与反歧视 | Workforce diversity and antidiscrimination

- **劳动力多元化（workforce diversity）**包括员工在人口特征、经历、能力和视角上的差异；年龄是常见的人口多元化维度，电脑技能或穿衣风格不是人口统计分类。**Workforce diversity** includes differences in employees’ demographic characteristics, experiences, abilities, and perspectives. Age is a common demographic dimension; computer skills and clothing style are not demographic categories.
- 同一族群中的个体并不必然拥有相同的外貌、语言、经历或文化传统。管理者不应根据群体标签推断个人特征。People in the same ethnic group do not necessarily share the same appearance, language, experiences, or cultural traditions. Managers should not infer an individual’s traits from group labels.
- 招聘、晋升、培训和解雇应依据岗位相关资格与一致、公平的标准。Recruitment, promotion, training, and termination should be based on job-related qualifications and consistent, fair standards.
- 试题引用的是美国法律体系；实际适用法规会因国家/地区而不同。The questions refer to the U.S. legal system; applicable laws differ by country and jurisdiction.
  - **《民权法案》第七章（Title VII of the Civil Rights Act）**禁止基于种族、肤色、宗教、原国籍和性别的就业歧视。**Title VII of the Civil Rights Act** prohibits employment discrimination based on race, color, religion, national origin, and sex.
  - **《美国残疾人法案》（Americans with Disabilities Act, ADA）**要求在适用范围内为合格残障员工提供合理便利；是否能胜任岗位应结合岗位基本职能与个案事实判断。The **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)** requires reasonable accommodation for qualified employees with disabilities where applicable. Ability to perform a job should be assessed in light of its essential functions and the facts of the case.
  - **《年龄歧视就业法》（Age Discrimination in Employment Act, ADEA）**保护其适用范围内的员工免受年龄歧视。
    The **Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA)** protects covered employees from age discrimination.

### 5. 性骚扰：定义、预防与处理 | Sexual harassment: definition, prevention, and response

- 性骚扰不是“任何涉及性内容的言语”这么简单；关键在于相关言语或身体行为是否**不受欢迎**，以及是否造成敌意或冒犯性工作环境、干扰工作，或影响就业机会。Sexual harassment is not simply “any speech with sexual content.” The key issues include whether the verbal or physical conduct is **unwelcome** and whether it creates a hostile or offensive work environment, interferes with work, or affects employment opportunities.
- 题目涉及的美国 EEOC 概述强调：不受欢迎的性相关言语或身体行为若造成敌意环境、干扰个人工作，或削弱其就业机会，可能构成性骚扰。The U.S. EEOC summary referenced by the qyuestions emphasizes that unwelcome sexual verbal or physical conduct may constitute harassment if it creates a hostile environment, interferes with an individual’s work, or adversely affects employment opportunities.
- 员工拒绝不当性要求后仍获晋升，并不自动证明没有骚扰；应调查不受欢迎的行为、权力关系、工作影响和适用法律。An employee’s subsequent promotion after rejecting an improper sexual request does not automatically prove that no harassment occurred. Investigate the unwelcome conduct, power relationships, workplace impact, and applicable law.
- 组织应建立清晰政策、可信的投诉渠道、及时公正的调查和纠正程序，并保护员工免受报复。Organizations should establish clear policies, trusted complaint channels, prompt and impartial investigations, corrective procedures, and protection from retaliation.
- 投诉应由未涉入事件且具备适当权限的人员审查；不能只由被投诉者本人决定，也不能因员工仍在职或得到某项机会就忽略投诉。A complaint should be reviewed by an appropriately authorized person who was not involved in the incident. It should not be decided solely by the accused person or dismissed because the employee remains employed or received an opportunity.
- 性骚扰可能造成员工身心伤害、团队信任下降、组织声誉受损以及重大诉讼和赔偿风险，因此预防和及时处理是管理责任。
  Sexual harassment can harm employees, weaken team trust, damage an organization’s reputation, and create significant litigation and compensation risks. Prevention and prompt response are therefore management responsibilities.

### 6. 缩编、临时用工与全球劳动力 | Downsizing, contingent work, and the global workforce

- **缩编（downsizing）**会影响被裁员工，也会影响留任员工及其家庭；留任者可能出现不安全感、士气下降、信任受损和工作负担增加。**Downsizing** affects employees who lose their jobs as well as survivors and their families. Survivors may experience insecurity, lower morale, reduced trust, and increased workloads.
- 缩编时应考虑沟通、法律合规、公平标准、离职支持，以及如何恢复留任员工的信任和工作能力。Downsizing should account for communication, legal compliance, fair criteria, transition support, and ways to restore survivors’ trust and capacity.
- **临时/应变劳动力（contingent workforce）**：按需要聘用的兼职、临时或合同员工。其灵活性较高，但组织仍需明确工作安排、待遇和适用法律责任。**Contingent workforce:** Part-time, temporary, or contract employees hired as needed. This workforce can provide flexibility, but organizations must clarify work arrangements, compensation, and applicable legal responsibilities.
- 全球协作和跨时区沟通可能模糊工作与非工作时间的界线；管理者应明确响应预期、工作时间和休息安排。Global collaboration and communication across time zones can blur the boundary between work and nonwork. Managers should clarify response expectations, working hours, and rest arrangements.
- 跨国 HRM 须考虑当地劳动法、员工参与机制和文化差异。例如德国的**工会/职工委员会（works councils）**参与某些人员决策；具体权利以当地现行法律为准。
  International HRM must account for local labor laws, employee-participation mechanisms, and cultural differences. For example, **works councils** in Germany participate in certain personnel decisions; specific rights depend on current local law.

### 7. 简答题：为什么性骚扰是当今职场中的难题？ | Short answer: Why is sexual harassment a difficult issue in today’s workplace?

可按“定义与边界—权力关系—受害者顾虑—组织责任—处理措施—后果”作答：性骚扰涉及不受欢迎的性相关行为及其对工作环境或就业机会的影响；权力差异可能使员工不敢报告；调查需兼顾保密、公平和防止报复。组织应以清晰政策、培训、独立投诉渠道、及时公正调查和一致问责来预防与处理，否则会伤害员工并引发声誉和法律风险。

Organize the answer around **definition and boundaries → power relationships → reporting concerns → organizational responsibility → response measures → consequences**. Sexual harassment involves unwelcome sexual conduct and its impact on the work environment or employment opportunities. Power differences may discourage reporting, while investigations must balance confidentiality, fairness, and protection from retaliation. Organizations should prevent and address harassment through clear policies, training, independent complaint channels, prompt impartial investigations, and consistent accountability; failure can harm employees and create reputational and legal risks.

## 简答题作答框架 | Short-answer frameworks

### A. 比较战略计划和战术计划 | Compare strategic and tactical plans

先定义二者，再比较层级、范围、时间跨度、制定者和具体程度。战略计划规定组织总体目标和方向；战术计划由部门/单位将战略转化为具体行动。最后说明战术目标应支持战略目标。

Define both plans, then compare their level, scope, time frame, authors, and specificity. Strategic plans set overall organizational goals and direction; tactical plans translate strategy into concrete departmental actions. Conclude by explaining that tactical goals should support strategic goals.

### B. 说明矩阵结构及其优缺点 | Explain the matrix structure and its pros and cons

按“结构定义 → 双重汇报关系 → 优点 → 缺点 → 适用情境/管理措施”组织答案。至少提到专业资源共享、跨职能协作，以及双重权威可能导致的冲突。

Organize the answer as: definition → dual reporting → advantages → disadvantages → suitable conditions/management practices. Mention shared specialist resources, cross-functional collaboration, and potential conflict from dual authority.

### C. 说明学习型组织的结构特征 | Describe the structural features of a learning organization

先定义持续学习和知识应用，再写扁平/灵活、权力下放、团队合作、信息共享、员工授权、支持试验与反思。结尾说明这些设计如何帮助组织适应变化。

Define continuous learning and knowledge application, then discuss a flatter and more flexible structure, decentralized authority, teamwork, information sharing, employee empowerment, experimentation, and reflection. Explain how these features help the organization adapt.

### D. 说明创造力与创新 | Explain creativity and innovation

区分“产生新颖有用想法”和“将想法实施并产生价值”；接着论述个人、团队和组织条件，最后用新产品、流程或服务改进举例。

Distinguish generating novel and useful ideas from implementing ideas to create value. Discuss individual, team, and organizational conditions, then give an example involving a new product or an improved process or service.

### E. 比较多国本土型、全球型与跨国型企业 | Compare multidomestic, global, and transnational corporations

按本地适应、全球整合/效率、决策权分配和知识流动四个维度比较。多国本土型重本地自主，全球型重统一协调，跨国型兼顾两类目标并促进跨国知识共享。

Compare local responsiveness, global integration/efficiency, decision authority, and knowledge flows. Multidomestic firms emphasize local autonomy; global firms emphasize coordination and standardization; transnational firms seek both and promote cross-border knowledge sharing.

### F. 分析变革情境 | Analyze a change scenario

按“变革来源 → 变革类型 → 阻力/风险 → 管理方法 → 如何巩固”作答。先判断外部或内部推动，再判断结构、技术或人员变革，最后用 Lewin 三阶段安排实施。

Use the sequence: forces of change → type of change → resistance/risks → management approach → reinforcement. Identify whether the force is internal or external, classify the change as structural, technological, or people-related, and organize implementation using Lewin’s three steps.

本模块依据近期加入的试题照片整理，照片中重复或重叠的题目已合并。以下为可直接用于复习的参考答案，不代表官方标准答案；考试时可按题目要求增删例子和细节。

### A. 管理基础 | Management foundations

#### 1. In a short essay, list and explain the four basic functions of management.

**题意：**列出并解释管理的四项基本职能。

**参考答案：**管理包括计划、组织、领导和控制。计划是确定目标并选择实现目标的行动方案；组织是安排任务、人员和资源，建立工作关系；领导是通过沟通、指导和激励影响员工；控制是衡量实际绩效、与目标比较，并在出现偏差时采取纠正措施。四项职能相互联系，控制结果也会为下一轮计划提供反馈。

**Model answer:** The four basic functions of management are planning, organizing, leading, and controlling. Planning sets goals and chooses actions to achieve them. Organizing arranges tasks, people, resources, and working relationships. Leading influences employees through communication, guidance, and motivation. Controlling measures actual performance, compares it with goals, and c orrects significant deviations. The functions are connected: control results also provide feedback for future planning.

#### 2. In a short essay, briefly discuss the importance of studying management.

**题意：**简要讨论学习管理的重要性。

**参考答案：**学习管理有助于理解组织如何协调人员和资源、如何实现共同目标，以及管理决策怎样影响员工和顾客。管理知识具有普遍性，适用于企业、政府和非营利组织；无论未来成为管理者还是作为被管理的员工，了解管理都能帮助人们更有效地沟通、合作和解决问题。它也能培养计划、决策和领导等职业技能，提升组织绩效。

**Model answer:** Studying management helps us understand how organizations coordinate people and resources, pursue shared goals, and affect employees and customers through managerial decisions. Management is useful in businesses, government agencies, and nonprofit organizations. Whether we become managers or work under them, this knowledge helps us communicate, cooperate, and solve problems more effectively. It also develops career skills such as planning, decision making, and leadership, which can improve organizational performance.

#### 3. In a short essay, explain why customer satisfaction is becoming increasingly important in today's business world.

**题意：**解释为什么顾客满意度在当今商业环境中日益重要。

**参考答案：**市场竞争、线上评价和信息传播使顾客更容易比较和更换供应商。满意的顾客更可能持续购买、保持忠诚并向他人推荐；不满意的体验则可能迅速损害企业声誉。关注满意度还可帮助企业从反馈中发现产品或服务问题，改进质量并建立差异化优势。因此，顾客满意既关系到顾客关系，也关系到长期竞争力，但并不意味着每个顾客要求都必须不计成本地满足。

**Model answer:** Competition, online reviews, and rapid information sharing make it easier for customers to compare providers and switch. Satisfied customers are more likely to buy again, remain loyal, and recommend a business, while poor experiences can quickly damage its reputation. Feedback about satisfaction also helps managers identify problems and improve products or services, creating a competitive advantage. Customer satisfaction therefore supports long-term customer relationships and competitiveness, although it does not mean meeting every request regardless of cost.

#### 4. In a short essay, explain why innovation is becoming increasingly important in today's business world.

**题意：**解释创新在当今商业环境中日益重要的原因。

**参考答案：**技术进步、全球竞争和顾客偏好的变化不断改变市场，产品和工作方法也可能很快过时。创新使组织能够开发新产品和服务、改进流程、提高生产率并回应新的顾客需求；它还帮助组织适应环境变化并形成竞争优势。创新并非只提出新点子，还需要评估可行性、投入适当资源并将想法有效实施。

**Model answer:** Technological progress, global competition, and changing customer preferences continually reshape markets, and products or work methods can become outdated quickly. Innovation allows organizations to develop products and services, improve processes, raise productivity, and respond to new customer needs. It also helps them adapt and build competitive advantage. Innovation involves more than generating ideas: managers must assess feasibility, commit suitable resources, and implement promising ideas effectively.

#### 5. In a short essay, compare the tasks, priorities, and responsibilities of first-line, middle, and top managers.

**题意：**比较基层、中层和高层管理者的任务、重点与职责。

| 管理层级                                 | 主要任务与重点                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **基层管理者 First-line managers** | 直接监督一线员工和日常运营，安排班次、处理即时问题、确保工作标准落实；重视短期执行，技术技能常较重要。         |
| **中层管理者 Middle managers**     | 管理部门或单位，将高层战略转化为具体计划，协调团队和 部门、分配资源并报告进度；连接战略与日常执行。            |
| **高层管理者 Top managers**        | 确定组织总体目标、战略和方向，处理重大外部关系及长期问题，配置关键资源并对整体绩效负责；概念技能通常尤其重要。 |

**Model answer:** First-line managers directly supervise employees and daily operations. They schedule work, address immediate problems, and ensure standards are followed; technical skills are often important. Middle managers lead departments or units, translate strategy into plans, coordinate teams, allocate resources, and report results. Top managers set organization-wide goals and strategy, address long-term and external issues, allocate major resources, and take responsibility for overall performance. The levels differ in scope and time horizon, but all need human skills and must coordinate work toward organizational goals.

#### 6. In a short essay, discuss the difference between efficiency and effectiveness.

**题意：**讨论效率与效果的区别。

**参考答案：**效率关注“怎样把事情做对”，即以较少的时间、成本和资源完成工作，减少浪费；效果关注“是否做对了该做的事”，即是否实现重要目标。例如，一家企业用更少原料生产同样数量的合格产品，提高了效率；若它生产的产品并不符合顾客需求，即使效率高也未必有效果。优秀管理要兼顾两者：实现正确目标，同时合理使用资源。

**Model answer:** Efficiency is about doing things right: completing work with less time, cost, or other resources and reducing waste. Effectiveness is about doing the right things: achieving important goals. For example, a company that uses fewer materials to produce the same number of acceptable products has improved efficiency. But if those products do not meet customer needs, the company may be efficient without being effective. Good management seeks to achieve the right goals while using resources wisely.

### B. 创造力、创新与决策 | Creativity, innovation, and decision making

#### 7. In a short essay, define and discuss creativity and innovation in the workplace.

**题意：**定义并讨论工作场所中的创造力与创新。

**参考答案：**创造力是产生新颖且有用想法的能力；创新是把这些想法转化为实际的新产品、服务、流程或工作方法。创造力提供可能的解决方案，创新则要求组织评估、选择并落实方案。例如，员工提出减少顾客排队的新办法是创造性想法；试行并推广有效的预约系统，才是将其转化为创新。组织应提供资源、协作机会和支持性氛围，使有价值的想法能够实施。

**Model answer:** Creativity is the ability to generate ideas that are both novel and useful. Innovation is the implementation of those ideas as valuable new products, services, processes, or work methods. Creativity supplies possible solutions; innovation requires an organization to evaluate, select, and put a solution into practice. For example, suggesting a new way to reduce customer queues is a creative idea; piloting and adopting an effective appointment system turns it into innovation. Organizations should provide resources, collaboration, and support so useful ideas can be implemented.

#### 8. In a short essay, discuss the three sets of variables that have been found to stimulate innovation.

**题意：**讨论能够促进创新的三类变量。

**参考答案：**三类变量是结构变量、文化变量和人力资源变量。结构方面，有机、灵活的组织结构、充足资源、跨部门沟通和较少不必要的时间压力，有利于创意交流和试验。文化方面，组织应容忍模糊和合理风险、接纳不同意见、减少过度控制，并鼓励开放讨论和建设性反馈。人力资源方面，招聘具有创造力的人才，持续提供培训与发展机会，并通过支持和适当的工作保障让员工愿意投入创新。三类条件需要共同发挥作用，单靠个人有创意并不足以保证创新。

**Model answer:** The three sets are structural, cultural, and human-resource variables. Structurally, an organic and flexible design, adequate resources, communication across units, and limited unnecessary time pressure can support idea sharing and experimentation. Culturally, organizations can encourage innovation by tolerating ambiguity and reasonable risk, welcoming disagreement, avoiding excessive control, and providing constructive feedback. In human-resource practices, organizations can recruit creative people, invest in training and development, and provide support and reasonable job security so employees are willing to contribute. These conditions work together; individual creativity alone does not guarantee innovation.

#### 9. In a short essay, identify and discuss two external forces that bring about the need for change.

**题意：**指出并讨论促使组织变革的两种外部力量。

**参考答案：**技术变化和竞争压力是两种重要的外部推动力。新技术可能改变生产方式、顾客服务和岗位所需技能，组织若不更新流程或培训员工，效率与服务质量可能落后。竞争者推出更低价或更便利的产品，也会改变顾客期望，促使组织调整定价、产品或服务方式。管理者应先判断外部变化对目标和能力的影响，再选择适当的结构、技术或人员变革。

**Model answer:** Technological change and competitive pressure are two important external forces. New technology can alter production, customer service, and the skills jobs require; without updated processes or training, an organization may fall behind in efficiency or service quality. Competitors may also introduce lower-priced or more convenient offerings, changing customer expectations and prompting changes in products, pricing, or service. Managers should assess how these forces affect organizational goals and capabilities before choosing structural, technological, or people-related changes.

#### 10. In a short essay, describe how electronic meetings can help avoid groupthink and enhance creativity in group decision making.

**题意：**说明电子会议如何帮助避免群体迷思并促进群体决策中的创造力。

**参考答案：**电子会议可让成员同时提交想法，并可采用匿名方式表达意见，从而减少职位较高或声音较大的成员主导讨论、他人担心被评价而保持沉默的情况。成员能并行提出更多方案，扩大信息和观点来源，有利于创造性思考。匿名和技术本身并不能保证没有群体迷思；主持者仍应鼓励异议、先独立思考、比较多种方案，并对重要决定进行复核。

**Model answer:** Electronic meetings can let members submit ideas simultaneously and, when appropriate, anonymously. This can reduce domination by senior or outspoken members and lessen the fear of being judged, allowing quieter members to contribute. Parallel input can produce more alternatives and a wider range of information, supporting creativity. However, anonymity and technology do not automatically prevent groupthink. A facilitator should still invite disagreement, allow independent thinking, compare alternatives, and review important decisions.

#### 11. In a short essay, discuss the differences among a procedure, a rule, and a policy. Include specific examples.

**题意：**讨论程序、规则和政策的区别，并举例说明。

**参考答案：**政策是指导决策的较宽泛原则，说明组织希望遵循的方向；规则是必须遵守或禁止违反的具体要求；程序则是完成某项工作的步骤顺序。例如，差旅政策可以要求员工选择经济、安全的出行方式；规则可以规定所有差旅费用须在返程后十个工作日内提交凭证；报销程序则依次说明如何填写申请、附上票据、交由主管审批并提交财务。简言之，政策指明方向，规则设定要求，程序说明怎么做。

**Model answer:** A policy is a broad guideline that directs decisions and states the organization’s preferred approach. A rule is a specific requirement that must be followed or a prohibition. A procedure is an ordered set of steps for completing a task. For example, a travel policy may ask employees to choose economical and safe travel; a rule may require receipts within ten business days after a trip; and a reimbursement procedure may explain how to complete a form, attach receipts, obtain a manager’s approval, and submit it to finance. In short, a policy guides, a rule specifies what is required, and a procedure explains how to act.

12. In a short essay, list and discuss the first four steps in the decision-making process. Give an example of each step.

**题意：**列出并讨论决策过程的前四步，每一步举例说明。

**参考答案：**以餐厅要减少午餐时段食物浪费为例：

1. **识别问题：**实际浪费量持续高于目标，明确问题是浪费过多，而不是预先认定某个员工或菜品有错。
2. **确定决策标准：**列出评估方案的标准，如减少浪费、顾客满意度、实施成本和操作便利性。
3. **分配标准权重：**确定各标准的重要程度，例如优先减少浪费和保持顾客满意，同时设定可接受的成本范围。
4. **提出备选方案：**考虑小份餐、预订制、按需求调整备餐量或捐赠未售出的安全食品等方案。
   这四步先界定问题，再确定如何衡量和有哪些可选办法；之后才分析并选择方案。

**Model answer:** Suppose a restaurant wants to reduce food waste at lunch.

1. **Identify the problem:** Waste is consistently above the target. Define excessive waste as the problem rather than assuming a particular employee or dish is at fault.
2. **Identify decision criteria:** List measures such as waste reduction, customer satisfaction, implementation cost, and operational convenience.
3. **Weight the criteria:** Decide how important each measure is; for example, prioritize waste reduction and customer satisfaction while setting an acceptable cost range.
4. **Develop alternatives:** Consider smaller portions, advance ordering, adjusting preparation to demand, or donating safe unsold food.
   These steps define the problem and establish how to assess possible responses before alternatives are analyzed and selected.

#### 13. In a short essay, discuss why creativity is important to decision making. Describe a situation in which a creative solution is used to solve a problem.

**题意：**讨论创造力对决策的重要性，并描述运用创造性方案解决问题的情境。

**参考答案：**创造力能扩大备选方案，帮助决策者跳出习惯做法，在信息不完整或问题新颖时找到更合适的解决办法。比如，校园餐厅午餐排队过长，传统做法是单纯增加收银员；管理者也可以设计手机预点餐和分时取餐，让顾客错峰领取。随后比较成本、使用便利性和排队时间，并小范围试行；若结果改善，再扩大实施。创造性提出方案之后，仍需理性评估其可行性和效果。

**Model answer:** Creativity expands the alternatives available and helps decision makers move beyond habitual responses, especially when a problem is new or information is incomplete. For example, if a campus cafeteria has long lunch queues, simply adding cashiers is not the only option. Managers might introduce mobile pre-ordering and timed pickup so customers collect meals at staggered times. They can compare cost, convenience, and waiting time, pilot the idea, and expand it if results improve. Creative ideas still need to be evaluated for feasibility and effectiveness.

### C. 人力资源管理 | Human resource management

#### 14. In a short essay, discuss why sexual harassment is such a difficult problem in today's workplace.

**题意：**讨论为什么性骚扰是当今工作场所中难以处理的问题。

**参考答案：**性骚扰可能发生在存在权力差距的关系中，受影响者可能担心报复、失去工作机会或不被相信，因此不愿报告。行为是否不受欢迎、是否造成敌意环境或影响工作机会，需要结合具体情境和证据谨慎判断；事件调查又涉及隐私、保密和公平程序。与此同时，忽视投诉可能继续伤害员工并损害团队信任、组织声誉和法律合规。组织应建立明确政策和可信的报告渠道，及时、公正地调查并保护报告者免受报复；不能把举证困难当作不处理的理由。

**Model answer:** Sexual harassment can occur in relationships with power imbalances, and affected employees may fear retaliation, lost opportunities, or not being believed. Whether conduct is unwelcome and creates a hostile environment or affects employment opportunities must be assessed carefully in context and with evidence; investigations also involve privacy, confidentiality, and fair process. At the same time, ignoring a complaint can cause further harm and damage trust, reputation, and legal compliance. Organizations need clear policies and trusted reporting channels, prompt and impartial investigations, and protection against retaliation. Difficulty establishing the facts is not a reason to dismiss a complaint.

#### 15. In a short essay, discuss how workforce diversity is directly affected by recruitment, selection, orientation, and training.

**题意：**讨论招聘、甄选、入职引导和培训如何直接影响劳动力多元化。

**参考答案：**招聘决定哪些群体能看到岗位并进入候选人池；拓宽招聘渠道有助于扩大候选人的来源。甄选应采用与岗位相关、前后一致的标准，减少无关偏见并公平评估资格。入职引导让新员工理解组织政策、文化、支持资源和包容性行为规范，影响其归属感与融入。培训可提升员工跨差异合作的能力，并确保不同员工公平获得发展机会。因此，多元化不仅取决于招到谁，也取决于甄选、融入和后续发展是否公平包容。

**Model answer:** Recruitment determines which groups learn about a job and enter the applicant pool; broader outreach can widen that pool. Selection should use consistent, job-related criteria so qualifications are assessed fairly and irrelevant bias is reduced. Orientation introduces new employees to organizational policies, culture, support resources, and inclusive norms, shaping their sense of belonging and integration. Training can strengthen employees’ ability to work across differences and ensure fair access to development. Workforce diversity therefore depends not only on whom an organization hires, but also on whether selection, integration, and development are inclusive.
